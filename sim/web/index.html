Drag the drift slider in free-running mode to see what two unsynchronised halves look like after a long uptime. Switch to synced and the drift is corrected away.

Power gate

WS2812s draw about 1 mA each even showing black, so a powered strip costs tens of milliamps with nothing lit. When a frame comes out entirely black the engine cuts the rail, and a lit frame brings it back.

Drag brightness to zero, or use the button, and watch the rail drop out after the delay. Then click a key: the ripple wakes it again.

Keyboard state

What the indicator layers read. On hardware these come from ZMK events; here you drive them, which is how a battery or profile indicator can be checked without draining a cell.

Caps lock is the host's state, not the keyboard's: on hardware it arrives as an LED report from the computer, which is why an indicator for it needs CONFIG_ZMK_HID_INDICATORS. The other half's battery starts at 0, meaning it has not reported yet — not that it is flat, which an indicator can show differently.
